Ignore every old number, minimum panel, rule, title and word in reference 2.
Style: vivid hand-painted graphic-novel / screenprint skateboard card art, warm cream concrete, deep navy metal edges, clear cobalt sky and white clouds, palms, turquoise lagoon, distant coastal city and mountain. Coherent sunlight and perspective. Rich illustrated texture, not 3D toy render, not CAD, not photorealistic.
Output framing: exactly one complete upright card, portrait 2:3, all four rounded corners visible with a narrow cream outside margin. No tabletop mockup, no hands, no second card. Full illustration fills almost all of the card beneath a COMPACT header and continues behind the translucent lower information panel. Use the compact header for the title on the left and an outlined navy circular TYPE-ICON badge on the upper right. The orange appearance ribbon sits immediately under the title. Preserve maximum artwork area; no huge title block or empty footer.

The victory panel has a navy top tab and translucent warm-cream body with large navy PV. NO MINIMUM or difficulty panel, no dice requirements, no invented bonus or rules text, no route labels, no arrows. Use symbols only for trick families, never their names. No skaters or people. No watermark.

Do not add or move obstacles, invent a different park, replace the tabletop with a halfpipe, or make quarterpipes convex. Only depict the relevant subset naturally visible from the specified camera.

Card text (verbatim): title "LE QUAI BAS"; orange ribbon "MANCHES 1–2"; victory "VICTOIRE" and "2 PV"; bottom "LE LAGON".
Type badge: one clear WHITE LOW GRIND RAIL symbol, with horizontal bar and two supports, on navy. No text within the badge.
Viewpoint and subject: camera at skater standing height, slightly elevated, near the SOUTHWEST low bank starting deck, looking NORTHEAST across the low long grind ledge. The long cream concrete ledge with navy metal grind edge is the HERO, large and clearly readable in the foreground, running diagonally away from lower left toward middle right. It is LOW and inviting for an early-game module. Keep enough open landing flat beside and beyond it; a small part of the starting bank lip can frame the lower left. The long wave-like southeast quarterpipe return is visible to the right beyond the ledge, and the central filled tabletop is farther behind, offset left of that return, as in the actual park. Preserve clear visual hierarchy: foreground ledge, open flat, supporting ramp in background. Ocean, palms and a small coastal skyline on the far north horizon tie it to Le Lagon. This is a close scene within the park, not the entire aerial map.
Mood: welcoming simple first line, sunlit cream surfaces and soft turquoise wave-painted sides. Keep the ledge shape and grind edge physically plausible.
